# T0 Conformance Discrepancies

T0 has no accepted, investigating, or expected-failure divergence from the
reviewed syntax contract.

- Skips: forbidden
- Retries: forbidden
- Quarantine: forbidden
- Expected failures: none
- Platform-specific semantic exceptions: none

Any future divergence must receive a stable ID, affected case IDs, impact,
owner, resolution state, and review date. Until the contract and independent
fixtures are updated in the same reviewed change, the divergence is a failing
conformance result rather than an exception.
