# V0 Conformance Discrepancies

V0 has no accepted, investigating, quarantined, retried, skipped, or
expected-failure divergence from the reviewed voicing contract.

- Accepted discrepancies: 0
- Skips and todos: forbidden
- Retries: forbidden
- Quarantine: forbidden
- Expected failures: none
- Platform-specific semantic exceptions: none
- Wall-time musical cutoffs: forbidden

Any future divergence requires a stable ID, affected fixture and trace IDs,
impact, owner, resolution state, and review date. Until the contract and its
independent authority are updated together, a divergence is a failing
conformance result rather than an exception.
